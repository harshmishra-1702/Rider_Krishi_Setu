# Product Requirement Document (PRD): KrishiSetu - Rider Application

## 1. Overview
KrishiSetu is an end-to-end agritech marketplace optimizing rural supply chains. The **Rider Application** is a specialized Flutter client for rural commercial drivers (auto-rickshaws, pickups, mini-trucks) operating B2B multi-stop milk-run pickups and drop-offs.

## 2. Target User & Context
- **Users**: Rural drivers operating vehicles ranging from 250 kg capacity to 10+ tonnes.
- **Environment**: Low-end Android devices, intermittent 2G/3G/4G connectivity, low digital literacy.
- **Language**: Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, English with Text-to-Speech (TTS).

## 3. Core Features & Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Onboarding & Vehicle Profiling
- Mobile OTP login + identity verification placeholder (`[Aadhaar Redacted]`, Driver's License upload).
- Heterogeneous Fleet Classification:
  - Micro (<250 kg: E-rickshaw / auto-cart)
  - Small (250 kg – 1.5 T: Tata Ace / Mahindra Bolero Maxi Truck)
  - Medium (1.5 T – 5 T: Ashok Leyland Dost / Eicher Pro)
  - Large (5 T – 10+ T: Heavy Commercial Reefer / Open Body)

### 3.2 Dynamic Route Dispatch & Acceptance
- Driver receives trip offers calculated by the backend HFVRP engine.
- Ride offer shows: Total payload weight, number of farm-gate pickups, final destination (Cold Storage / Mandi / Buyer), estimated duration, and guaranteed payout.
- Turn-by-turn routing with optimized stop order.

### 3.3 Digital Chain of Custody & Verification
- **Farm-Gate Pickup**: Driver scans harvest batch QR code generated by farmer; app confirms batch weight & grade; state transitions to `IN_TRANSIT`.
- **Drop-off Point Handover**:
  - Driver must be within 100 meters of the buyer's geo-coordinates (Geofence Check).
  - Driver captures a mandatory photo of unloaded produce.
  - Driver enters the 4-digit buyer-generated OTP.

### 3.4 Fair Ton-Kilometer Earnings & Escrow Settlement
- Instant wallet update reflecting Ton-Kilometer cost share:
  $$C_i = \text{Total Trip Cost} \times \left(\frac{W_i \times D_i}{\sum_{j=1}^n (W_j \times D_j)}\right)$$
- Payout triggers automatically from escrow escrow pot to driver's UPI/bank account.

### 3.5 Offline-First Resilience
- Background location caching in SQLite during network blackouts.
- Automatic batch sync with backend once 4G/Wi-Fi connection is restored.
